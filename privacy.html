Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 26, 2026
Operator: Dakshina Murthy Karra, a sole proprietor based in Fremont, CA, USA
Contact: karrad@karra.co

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information needed to send personal SMS notifications to people who have consented to receive them:

  • Mobile phone number — provided directly by you in person, via email, phone call, or messaging app
  • Name — to address messages and maintain personal context
  • Message content — the text of SMS messages exchanged with you

We do not collect financial information, government identifiers, location data, browsing data, or biometric data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your phone number and name are used solely to send you personal SMS notifications, reminders, scheduling updates, and similar informational messages that you have agreed to receive. We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making.

3. SMS Consent and Mobile Information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

SMS messaging is delivered via Twilio Inc., a regulated communications provider acting as our service provider, not as an independent recipient of your data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information. We do not share your phone number, opt-in status, or any related data with advertisers, data brokers, affiliates, or any third party for any purpose other than delivering the messages you requested.

The only third party with access to your phone number is our SMS service provider (Twilio), which is contractually bound to use the data only to deliver the messages on our behalf and to comply with U.S. communications law.

5. Opt-Out and Your Rights

You may opt out of all SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive. Opt-outs are honored immediately and confirmed via a final SMS.

You may also request deletion of your phone number and any associated records by emailing karrad@karra.co. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

6. Data Retention

We retain your phone number only as long as you remain an active consenting recipient. Once you opt out, we retain a record of the opt-out (your number plus the STOP timestamp) for the sole purpose of honoring your opt-out, as required by law. We do not retain message history beyond what is incidentally stored by our SMS service provider for delivery and compliance purposes.

7. Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted transport (HTTPS, TLS) for any data in transit and access controls on stored data.

8. Children

This service is not directed to anyone under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page if our practices change. Material changes will be communicated to active recipients via SMS prior to taking effect.

10. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or your data: karrad@karra.co
